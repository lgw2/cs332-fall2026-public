In this activity, you will trace through the algorithm whatever-first-search. 

\begin{verbatim}
WFS(s):
	put s into the bag
	while the bag is not empty:
		take v from the bag
		if v is unmarked:
			mark v
			for each edge {v,w}:
				put w into the bag
\end{verbatim}
